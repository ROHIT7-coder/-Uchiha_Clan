Who We Are

About The Clan

Uchiha Clan is a Roblox community built around The Strongest Battlegrounds — formed for players who want more than casual matches. We train together, compete in tournaments, and push each other to get stronger every day.

Our home base is Discord, where members talk strategy, queue up for matches, and stay plugged into everything happening in the clan. Our Instagram is where we share clips, highlights, and clan culture with the wider TSB community.

Whether it's competitive 1v1s, tournament brackets, or just hanging out between matches, Uchiha Clan is about friendly competition and real clan growth — built different, and fighting together.
